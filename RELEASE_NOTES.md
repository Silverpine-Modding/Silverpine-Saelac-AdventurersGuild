# Adventurers’ Guild 1.5.2

Appoint a guild representative, post gold-backed commissions, and take requests from named residents. Enrolled adventurers sign in person and travel to their assignments. Companion Party adds personal rewards and combat on loaded tiles.

- Ordinary escort travelers resume their schedule where they arrive in Silverpine. You can collect the reward later. Rescued adventurers resume their interrupted assignments.
- An older unpaid escort that still holds its traveler at the wilderness rendezvous releases them in Silverpine on load.
- Ordinary enemies load quietly when their save record has no bounty marker. Wanted enemies retain their contract ID.

Requires Silverpine with BepInEx 5 and Silverpine Modding Tools 1.13.0 or later. Extract `AdventurersGuild-1.5.2.zip` into the game directory or place the standalone DLL in `BepInEx/plugins/AdventurersGuild/`.
